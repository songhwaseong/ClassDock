# Xylophone sample attribution

The xylophone samples in this directory are derived from the `xylophone`
instrument in [`tonejs-instruments`](https://github.com/nbrosowsky/tonejs-instruments)
by Nicholaus P. Brosowsky. The upstream `sample-source-info.txt` identifies the
source as VSCO 2 Community Edition.

The samples are distributed under the
[Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 license](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/).
They were selected for the score editor's G3-C6 range and are redistributed
without further audio editing.
