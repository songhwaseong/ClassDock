# Piano sample attribution

The piano samples in this directory are derived from the `piano` instrument in
[`tonejs-instruments`](https://github.com/nbrosowsky/tonejs-instruments) by
Nicholaus P. Brosowsky. The upstream sample source identifies the piano as
Versilian Studios Chamber Orchestra 2 (VSCO 2).

The samples are distributed under the
[Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported license](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/).

Only ten MP3 notes covering the application's G3–C6 score range are bundled.
They are played at adjusted rates for the pitches between recorded notes. No
audio editing is performed by this project beyond decoding and playback-rate
pitch adjustment at runtime.
